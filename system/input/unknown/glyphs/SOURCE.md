# Glyph art

The PNGs in these folders come from Nicolae "Xelu" Berbece's [Free Controller & Key Prompts](https://thoseawesomeguys.com/prompts/) pack, released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Credit is welcome but not required. Art for a game's own sets can come from the same pack to match.

| Folder | Pack folder |
| --- | --- |
| `mouse` | `Keyboard & Mouse/Dark`, and `simple.png` from `Keyboard & Mouse/Blanks` |
| `playstation` | `PS5`; `share.png` from `Others/PS4`, and the face buttons from `Others/Positional Prompts` |
| `steam_controller` | `Others/Steam`; `steam.png` is `Steam_System.png` |
| `steam_deck` | `Steam Deck`; `view.png` is `SteamDeck_Inventory.png` and `steam.png` is `SteamDeck_Steam.png` |
| `switch` | `Switch` |
| `xbox` | `Xbox Series`; the bumpers and sticks from `Others/Amazon Luna`, and `back.png` from `Others/Xbox 360` |

The PlayStation face buttons are drawn by position rather than with Sony's shapes. The keyboard arrows and `unknown.svg` are drawn for kit and fall under its license.
